EMBER DUEL

A duel of four elements. Outwit Rook, the Frost Warden, before he freezes your last ember.

⚔️ Reduce Rook to 0 HP to win. Both duelists start at 30 HP.

◆ Mana grows each turn (1, then 2, up to 10) and refills at the start of your turn.

🃏 Cards cost mana. Fire burns, Frost shields, Leaf heals, Storm draws and strikes.

🖱️ Click a card or press 1–7 to play it. Space ends your turn.

An original card game · plays fully offline · no account, no network
